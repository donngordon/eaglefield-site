Field service management

Field service software that closes every job right.

Eagle Field is field service management software for trade contractors. It covers the full job lifecycle and is built first for HVAC, with job steps and pricebooks for plumbing, electrical and flood-sensor installers too.

Request a private demoSee pricing · from $19/tech

The full job lifecycle

  1. Book the call with customer, equipment and history on screen.
  2. Dispatch a verified job summary to the right technician.
  3. Track the technician on the way, with a live link for the customer.
  4. Work the job step by step, with photos filed into each step.
  5. Debrief and close out before the next job starts.
  6. Invoice with pricebook and member pricing; take card or check on site.

Trades supported

  • HVAC (built first)
  • Plumbing
  • Electrical
  • Smart home and flood-sensor installation

Security and data

Data is stored on Amazon Web Services in the United States, encrypted and backed up daily, with each company's data kept separate at the database level. Technicians never see customers' personal phone numbers.

What is field service management software?

Field service management (FSM) software schedules and dispatches technicians, tracks jobs in the field, and handles invoicing, payments and customer history for service businesses.

Is Eagle Field only for HVAC?

It is built first for HVAC and also supports plumbing, electrical and flood-sensor installation.

Most shops can start in days. Founding customers get their data imported for them.
