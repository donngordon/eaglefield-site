Field service CRM for the trades. Set up your workspace once: name it, pick your trade, and Eagle Field builds the job steps your technicians document.

What Eagle Field does that others don't

Debrief gate before the next job

Every ticket ends with a video walkthrough or a call-in debrief. The manager closes it out, and only then does the tech's next job unlock.

Verified call summaries

Every line of the work order is checked against the customer's own words. Anything that doesn't match blocks dispatch until the dispatcher fixes it.

Snap once, every step fills itself

Techs take photos as they work. Each photo lands in the right step tab with a caption.

Every part tracked shop to van to customer

Scan with the phone camera. Each move is logged with who, when and a photo, and the price always comes from one pricebook record.

24/7 water monitoring with remote shut-off

Sensors at the drain pan report to your desk. Water shows up, the system shuts off, a verified repair job is created, and the customer gets a text.

Plus everything you expect: scheduling, customers and equipment, estimates, invoices, payments, customer texts and reports.
