FAQ

Questions contractors ask us.

How is Eagle Field different from ServiceTitan or Jobber?

Eagle Field is built around the mistakes that cost contractors money: wrong dispatches, skipped paperwork, second truck rolls and water damage nobody caught. It also costs less at every team size from 1 to 30 technicians, with no setup fee and no contract. Business owners can see exactly how it works in a private demo.

How much does Eagle Field cost?

Starter is $19 per technician a month, Pro $29 and Elite $35, with monthly caps of $199, $299 and $449. There's no setup fee and no contract, and paying yearly gets two months free.

Is there a setup fee or long contract?

No. Eagle Field is month to month with no setup fee.

Does it work for plumbing and electrical too?

Yes. Eagle Field is built for HVAC first and also supports plumbing, electrical and smart home and flood sensor installers, with job steps and a pricebook for each trade.

Where is my data stored?

On Amazon Web Services in the United States, encrypted, backed up daily, with each company's data kept separate at the database level.
